About the Studio

Stone & Glaze began with a simple idea: make useful objects that are beautiful to hold. Our pieces are wheel-thrown, bisque-fired, and hand-glazed using food-safe glazes. We accept small commission runs for restaurants and bespoke gifts.

  • Wheel-thrown cups, bowls, plates
  • Small-run collections & restaurantware
  • Workshops for beginners and intermediates
